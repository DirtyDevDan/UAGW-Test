Code of Conduct

Guild rules

Support the team, communicate with respect, and help make United Azeroth a community where people want to stay.

Raid standards

  • Sign up before the posted deadline and choose the correct role.
  • Arrive 15 minutes early with repaired gear and consumables.
  • Listen during strategy calls and keep combat comms clear.
  • Tell the Raid Officer as early as possible if you cannot attend.

Mythic+ standards

  • List the key level and dungeon when forming a group.
  • Be honest about experience and welcome learning runs.
  • Complete the agreed route unless the group decides together to change it.
  • Keep feedback constructive and never leave without communicating.

Core values

  • Support each other—this is your team.
  • Ask for help when you need it.
  • Help others when you can.

Guild events

  • Discord is required; listening is the minimum.
  • Event leaders have final say on participation.
  • Respect leadership and listen during calls.

Communication

  • No offensive or derogatory speech.
  • Be aware of tone, timing, and context.
  • Avoid spam, politics, and disruptive side conversations.

Voice etiquette

  • Use push-to-talk when needed.
  • Mute during meals or persistent background noise.
  • Do not interrupt; use side channels for group content.

Harassment & conduct

  • No bullying, harassment, or discrimination.
  • If asked to stop, stop.
  • Aggressive behavior may result in immediate removal.

Discipline

  • First violation: official warning.
  • Second violation: removal from the guild and Discord.
  • Severe misconduct may skip the warning stage.

Reporting issues

  • Report concerns privately to guild leadership.
  • All parties will be reviewed fairly.
  • Leadership aims to decide within seven days.

Community integrity

  • No recruiting members for outside communities.
  • No repeated unsolicited invitations to other groups.
  • Bring new community ideas to leadership.

Guild bank

  • Ask an officer for specific items.
  • Donations are voluntary and are not reimbursed.
  • Items may be returned when still available.

Advertising

  • Do not charge guild members for carries.
  • Use trade chat to sell services.
  • Transactions are conducted at your own risk.

Membership

  • Alt characters require approval.
  • Returning members should contact leadership.
  • Membership may be ended to protect the community.

Inclusivity

  • We support players of all backgrounds.
  • Request reasonable accommodations when needed.
  • Contact leadership privately for support.

A reminder from Kold's Dad

Keep the spirit alive

Have fun making friends, work together as a team, and enjoy the effort that goes into making this community a thriving environment.
